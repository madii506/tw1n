04 · how it works

Twins feed. Vamps drain.

01

Pick

Pick any pump.fun coin. TWIN lets each coin have one twin, first come, so no one can pile copies on it here.

02

Launch

Your twin launches on pump.fun with its bonding curve priced in the pair, not in SOL. pump.fun stores the pair in the curve itself.

03

Buy

Every buy walks SOL › pair › twin in one pump.fun swap. The buyer buys the pair first, then pays it into the twin's curve.

04

Hold

The pair the buyers paid sits in the twin's curve. The more the twin is bought, the more of the pair it holds.

05 · the fine print

Questions.

Open a twin and look at its curve before you buy.

What is a twin?

A pump.fun coin whose bonding curve is priced in another pump.fun coin, its pair, instead of SOL. pump.fun supports these curves natively and stores the pair inside the curve, so the pairing is on-chain and permanent.

Why does it stop vamps?

A vamp copies a coin and pulls buyers away from it. A twin is priced in the coin it copies, so every buy of the twin buys the original first. On TWIN each coin can have one twin, first come, so copies cannot pile on it here.

What happens when I buy?

One transaction: pump.fun's multi-hop swap turns your SOL into the pair and the pair into the twin. You sign it in your own wallet. TWIN simulates the swap first and sets a minimum out, so you never get less than shown minus slippage.

And when I sell?

The same swap in reverse: twin into the pair, the pair into SOL. Selling a twin sells the pair it gets back, so the twin moves with its pair in both directions.

Can a twin have a twin?

Yes. A twin is a pump.fun coin too, so it can be paired, as deep as pump.fun allows for quote chains.

Who holds my coins?

You do. TWIN builds each transaction and your wallet signs it. TWIN never holds a key, a coin or a lamport. Every twin is listed from an on-chain memo and checked against its own curve: the creator and the pair must match.

What does it cost?

pump.fun's normal fees on each trade and Solana network fees. TWIN takes nothing.
